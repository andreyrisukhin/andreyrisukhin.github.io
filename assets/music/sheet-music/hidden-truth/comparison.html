Reading and playing the variants

  • M means your clean middle reed. H means the higher octave reed. Detuned middle means a small tuning offset, not a semitone.
  • Left-hand octave 2 is representative notation for root buttons. Your high/middle/low register determines the real sound. These scores do not require free bass.
  • In C, labelled stacked left-hand notes mean one preset chord button, not separate free-bass notes. The displayed and synthesized chord voicing is illustrative.
  • C's written right-hand notes in m.20–52 are fingering pitches for M+H registration. Ordinary MusicXML readers may play only the written note. The supplied MIDI and this player include octave coupling, plus a +10-cent illustrative reed at the climax.
  • A and B deliberately avoid adding guessed preset chords to the accompaniment. C is a harmonic reduction: extended colours in some G♭ passages are simplified to G♭ major. Ambiguous or suspended passages outside the listed chord substitutions retain the root-button pitch-class sequence.
  • All three omit the additional A in the original left-hand D–A–D sonority in m.3–4. All retain the source D-natural opening, G-natural in m.18, and E♭/F ending.
  • No dim7 button is used merely because it is available. Register names are suggestions; exact switches and bellows directions need a play-through on your instrument.
