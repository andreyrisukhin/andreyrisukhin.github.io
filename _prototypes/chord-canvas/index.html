Chord canvas

Type a chord and press Enter. Click anywhere to add another. Drag chords to arrange them. Arrow keys choose a neighboring cell; Shift and arrow keys move a chord. Enter edits a selected chord. Delete leaves an empty timed span. Drag empty space to pan.

Click anywhere. Type a chord. Enter to fill.

Drop onto a chord to split just that span in half. Drop onto an empty span to fill it. Neighbors keep their timing.

Select a span and choose Split… for thirds, fifths, or a ratio such as 2:1. Moving or clearing a chord leaves an empty timed span.

Drag a divider to resize its two neighbors. Focus a divider and use arrow keys for keyboard resizing.

Use + or Zoom to selected span to work on small subdivisions. Select any span with Tab, even when it is too narrow to click.

Drag the paper to pan. Scroll or use a trackpad, too.

Double-click a tile to edit. Arrow keys choose a cell; Shift + arrows move it.

Colors follow the root around the circle of fifths; chord quality changes the shade.

Empty canvas is untimed space; outlined empty spans belong to a measure.
