Chord canvas

Type a chord and press Enter. Click anywhere to add another. Drag chords to arrange them. Arrow keys choose a neighboring cell; Shift and arrow keys move a chord. Enter edits a selected chord. Delete removes it. Drag empty space to pan.

Click anywhere. Type a chord. Enter to fill.

Drop a chord onto another to squeeze them into two halves, 2 beats each. Drop on the left or right to choose the order.

Drag a half out to separate them into full 4-beat cells again. Each space holds at most two chords for now.

Drag the paper to pan. Scroll or use a trackpad, too.

Double-click a tile to edit. Arrow keys choose a cell; Shift + arrows move it.

Colors follow the root around the circle of fifths; chord quality changes the shade.
