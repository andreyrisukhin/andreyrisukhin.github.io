About this mockup

Pixel books have stepped cover boards, metal corners, and tiny source-inspired sprites. Cloth books have rounded spines, woven covers, and stamped line drawings. Colors are interpretations of each song’s world, not sampled artwork.

Thickness exaggerates the difference between short and long scores (8–79 bars), above a readable minimum. Width keeps all books equally thick and uses score length for the spine’s width instead. Games and Pop describe a source; Autumn describes a mood, so these are piles rather than a strict taxonomy.
