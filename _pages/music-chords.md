Abbreviations:

- M = major triad
- m = minor triad
- dim = diminished triad
- aug = augmented triad
- maj7 = major seventh
- 7 = dominant seventh
- m7 = minor seventh
- ø7 = half-diminished seventh
- °7 = fully diminished seventh
- +maj7 = augmented major seventh
