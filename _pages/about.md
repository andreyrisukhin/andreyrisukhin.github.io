I'm at [Factory AI](https://factory.ai/) in San Francisco, working on coding agents that learn from how people use them. My research interest is implicit feedback — inferring what a user wants from how they behave, not what they say. If you work on related problems, reach me via the icons below.

Previously at [Perceptron AI](https://www.perceptron.inc/) on multimodal video data, and [Contextual AI](https://contextual.ai/) on retrieval-augmented generation. B.S. and M.S. from the [University of Washington](https://www.washington.edu/). Deferred a CS Ph.D. at [UIUC](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) under [Tal August](https://talaugust.github.io/) indefinitely.
